## Larger files: current contract and tradeoffs

Photo desktop/Android: 512 MiB input, 200 MP, 32768px dimension ceiling; iOS Auto 24 MP. Large preview bounded to 1600px/2.56 MP; inference 256x256 cached colour transfer, not full-resolution neural restoration. Full export is source decode plus full output canvas, strip working buffers and encoded Blob. RAW/HEIC fallback decoder allocations can precede resizing. Reduced mode trades detail for memory; Full already lets capable devices exceed Auto within guards. Keep existing defaults until measured evidence supports change.

Video: 10 minutes, 34 MP/frame; input 512 MiB desktop/Android, 256 MiB iOS. Preview bounded 960x540. Compact up to 1080p/30fps/256 MiB; Source retains dimensions/timing subject to codec checks and output caps (512 MiB desktop/Android, 256 MiB iOS). Native bridge chunks bound transport only. Streaming encoded output to destination would reduce retained bytes but requires seekable mux/storage integration, cancellation cleanup and platform-specific download/share fallbacks. Tiled photo encoding likewise requires replacing whole-canvas encoding; global fit and spatial halos must remain identical to preview.

Adaptive policy proposal (not implemented): retain conservative Auto and explicit Reduced/Full; estimate concurrent surfaces from dimensions/decoder/engine, serialize large work, respond to actual allocation/codec failures with reduced retry. Never allocation-probe to exhaustion or treat reported RAM as free RAM. Bounded previews reduce editing cost, not full export cost. Avoid changing finish math or model defaults for speed without quality review.
