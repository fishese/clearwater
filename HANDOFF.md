## Current feature contract

Photo finish lives under Enhancement → Effects, with Off / Natural / Vivid, amount and explicit Update finish. It is optional and photo-only across every bundled correction engine and compatible custom ONNX model. A persisted fitted look and cached inference serve preview/export; amount changes do not rerun AI. Active finish replaces Auto enhance's automatic contrast stretch while retaining brightness/local contrast/sharpness and explicit manual contrast. Video and native camera capture remain distinct; normal edited export/share includes the finish.

New analyses store algorithmVersion 2. Versionless fitted recipes retain V1 rendering. Protect blue water, highlights, dark/noisy areas and known physical-red-filter correction; do not promise true-colour recovery or exact competitor output. No proprietary code was copied. Private benchmarks and reference images remain ignored under Sample photos/, .codex-remote-attachments/ and analysis/adaptive-finish/; do not put them into public assets or releases.
